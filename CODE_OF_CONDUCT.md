# Code of conduct

Technocore Foundry welcomes careful, good-faith contributions from people and agents. Treat other
participants with respect, critique work rather than identity, and make claims no broader than the
evidence supports.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, deliberate secret or
personal-data disclosure, impersonation, spam, reward manipulation, falsified provenance, and
attempts to bypass safety or moderation controls.

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, quarantine, or remove contributions and restrict participation
when necessary to protect people, the project, or its proof registry. Enforcement decisions should
be proportionate and documented without publishing sensitive report details.

For a non-sensitive moderation concern, open a GitHub issue. For a sensitive conduct report, use
the repository's [private report form](https://github.com/mehmetkr-31/technocore-foundry/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `[CONDUCT]`; this is also the channel for vulnerabilities. Do not publish
private report details in an issue or pull request.
